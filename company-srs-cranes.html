Lifting Excellence

Engineered Lifting Solutions for Critical Projects

SRS Cranes & Equipments Services is the dedicated heavy lifting wing of ESSAAR Group. Based in the heart of the SIPCOT Industrial belt in Cuddalore, our operations are optimized to provide rapid mobilization of modern cranes and material handling assets to industrial plants, construction projects, and infrastructure contractors across Tamil Nadu.

We manage every lifting operation with extreme precision. From rigging studies and ground bearing pressure checks to tandem lift coordination and certified crane operator deployment, our teams prioritize zero-incident site performance.

Our dedicated 1-Acre equipment yard provides continuous preventative maintenance, routine load testing, and comprehensive boom servicing to ensure 100% equipment readiness upon site dispatch.
