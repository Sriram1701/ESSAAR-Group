Enterprise Profile

Reliable Mechanical Contractors & Workforce Partners

ESSAAR Engineering & Company fulfills a crucial role within ESSAAR Group by executing intensive site mechanical works, pipeline interconnects, equipment erection, and large-scale industrial manpower deployment.

Whether handling an urgent scheduled shutdown for a continuous process chemical plant, executing critical pipeline ties, or relocating heavy factory machinery, our teams combine deep technical acumen with flexible labour mobilization.

All deployed tradesmen — from Tig/Arc welders and structural fabricators to pipe fitters and millwrights — are vetted for skill competency, safety compliance, and medical fitness prior to site assignment.
