# ESSAAR GROUP OF COMPANIES

> **One Group. Many Strengths. One Vision.**

A modern, responsive corporate conglomerate website for **ESSAAR Group of Companies**, showcasing the group's five specialized subsidiaries across industrial engineering, heavy lifting, industrial & medical gas supply, and fabrication solutions in Tamil Nadu and Puducherry, India.

---

## 🏢 Group Subsidiaries

1. **Prateesh Engineering Enterprises** — Industrial Engineering, Heavy Fabrication, Piping, Structural Steel & Turnkey Project Solutions (SIPCOT, Cuddalore).
2. **SRS Cranes & Equipments Services** — Heavy Lifting Solutions, Mobile Crane Rental (5–500 Tonnes Capacity) & Project Logistics.
3. **Grace Gases & Company** — Industrial, Medical & Speciality Gas Manufacturing, Cylinder Testing & Filling.
4. **Best Air Products** — Industrial & Medical Gas Distribution, Liquid Oxygen/Nitrogen/Argon & Bulk Supply Logistics.
5. **ESSAAR Engineering & Company** — Industrial Mechanical Maintenance, Plant Erection, Shutdown Services & Technical Manpower Supply.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Brand Design System:** High-end Dark Navy (`#081A33`) and Premium Gold (`#C9A34E`) color theme.
- **Enterprise Group Structure:** Interactive subsidiary overview and dedicated profile pages for each group company.
- **Double-Line Infinite Marquee Slider:** Animated running showcase of major client partners (L&T, Sanmar, Tata, Hyundai, Ramco, SAIL, Saint-Gobain, SPIC, Kansai Paint).
- **Executive Statistics Bar:** Four-column glassmorphic metric bar displaying group strength, capacity, and regional footprint with single-line precision.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Comprehensive technical and corporate questions with smooth expand/collapse.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Design:** Fully optimized drawer navigation and fluid layouts tested from 320px up to 4K displays.
- **SEO & Accessibility:** Clean semantic HTML5, accessible SVG icons, meta tags, OpenGraph data, schema-ready structure, `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt`.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **HTML5:** Semantic architecture with modern tags.
- **CSS3:** Custom CSS variables, responsive CSS Grid, Flexbox, glassmorphism, and hardware-accelerated animations.
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** Zero dependencies, lightweight DOM controllers for mobile drawer, sticky header, and accordions.
