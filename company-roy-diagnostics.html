Medical Technology

Proposed Diagnostic Imaging Services

ROY DIAGNOSTICS is developing modern diagnostic infrastructure across Cuddalore and surrounding districts,
with all listed equipment and modalities planned for clinical commissioning upon regulatory approvals.

Proposed Capability

1.5 Tesla MRI Imaging

Planned wide-bore 1.5 Tesla magnetic resonance imaging suite designed for comprehensive neuro, musculoskeletal, spine, abdominal and angiographic studies with advanced clinical protocols.

Proposed Capability

32-Slice CT Imaging

Planned multi-slice CT scanning system delivering rapid, low-dose volumetric imaging for emergency trauma triage, chest examinations, oncology staging, and vascular evaluations.

Proposed Capability

Digital X-Ray

Planned modern digital radiography system with flat-panel detectors ensuring low radiation exposure, rapid image acquisition, and high skeletal/thoracic diagnostic resolution.

Proposed Capability

Ultrasound & Colour Doppler

Planned high-end diagnostic ultrasound with high-resolution probes for vascular Doppler, abdominal, pelvic, musculoskeletal, small parts, and soft tissue assessments.

Specialized Domain

Fetal Medicine & Women's Imaging

Specialized maternal-fetal imaging infrastructure providing early pregnancy scans, detailed anomaly scans, fetal well-being evaluations, and comprehensive women's healthcare sonography.

Proposed Capability

Mammography

Planned dedicated low-dose breast imaging technology designed for preventive women's wellness, screening evaluations, and precise clinical diagnostic clarity.
