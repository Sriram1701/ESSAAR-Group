Medical Technology

Proposed Diagnostic Imaging Services

ROY DIAGNOSTICS is actively developing modern diagnostic infrastructure to serve patients, clinicians, and hospitals across Cuddalore and surrounding districts. All listed equipment and modalities are planned for clinical commissioning upon regulatory approvals.

Proposed Capability

1.5 Tesla MRI Imaging

Planned wide-bore 1.5 Tesla magnetic resonance imaging suite designed for comprehensive neuro, musculoskeletal, spine, abdominal and angiographic studies with advanced clinical protocols.

Proposed Capability

32-Slice CT Imaging

Planned multi-slice CT scanning system delivering rapid, low-dose volumetric imaging for emergency trauma triage, chest examinations, oncology staging, and vascular evaluations.

Proposed Capability

Digital X-Ray

Planned modern digital radiography system with flat-panel detectors ensuring low radiation exposure, rapid image acquisition, and high skeletal/thoracic diagnostic resolution.

Proposed Capability

Ultrasound & Colour Doppler

Planned high-end diagnostic ultrasound with high-resolution probes for vascular Doppler, abdominal, pelvic, musculoskeletal, small parts, and soft tissue assessments.

Specialized Domain

Fetal Medicine & Women's Imaging

Specialized maternal-fetal imaging infrastructure providing early pregnancy scans, detailed anomaly scans, fetal well-being evaluations, and comprehensive women's healthcare sonography.

Proposed Capability

Mammography

Planned dedicated low-dose breast imaging technology designed for preventive women's wellness, screening evaluations, and precise clinical diagnostic clarity.

Regulatory Compliance & Operational Standards

Development Status: ROY DIAGNOSTICS is currently under development. All equipment descriptions, imaging modalities, and clinical services listed are proposed and planned for deployment upon completion of facility infrastructure, equipment installation, calibration, and receipt of all applicable central, state, and local regulatory approvals and licensures.

Statutory Compliance (PCPNDT Act): In strict adherence to the Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques (Prohibition of Sex Selection) Act, 1994 (PCPNDT Act) and associated rules, all ultrasound and fetal medicine diagnostic examinations will be conducted in full compliance with mandated statutory registrations, documentation, and reporting protocols. Sex determination or disclosure of the sex of the fetus is strictly prohibited by law and is not performed.
