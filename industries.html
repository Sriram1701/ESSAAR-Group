Industries We Serve

ESSAAR Group supports critical core industrial sectors through integrated engineering fabrication,
heavy crane rental, pure industrial gases, and shutdown workforce solutions.

Sector 01

Chemical & Petrochemical

Chemical plants operate under rigorous temperature and corrosion regimes requiring ASME-compliant pressure vessels, specialized alloy steel piping, continuous nitrogen gas inerting, and high-capacity cranes for column changeouts.

Group Services Applied: 4000 KL storage tanks, chemical reactor vessels, nitrogen blanketing gas, plant turnarounds.
Key References: Chemplast Sanmar Ltd, Chemplast Cuddalore Vinyls, Crimsun Organics.
Sector 02

Manufacturing & Process Industries

Continuous manufacturing plants demand ongoing mechanical reliability, robust exhaust ducting, high-pressure utility lines, regular welding gases, and equipment handling machinery to sustain production quotas.

Group Services Applied: Heavy industrial ducting, machinery placement, compressed gas cylinder refills, AMC contracts.
Key References: Kansai Nerolac Paints, Hyundai component manufacturers, SPIC.
Sector 03

Power & Energy

Thermal and renewable energy projects involve heavy structural steel components, tall flue gas chimneys, high-pressure steam line installations, and massive mobile cranes for boiler turbine installation.

Group Services Applied: Chimney flue can fabrication (L&T project), structural towers, up to 500-Tonne heavy lifting.
Key References: Larsen & Toubro Ltd (L&T), power generation subcontractors.
Sector 04

Infrastructure & Construction

Highway bridges, metro flyovers, industrial park sheds, and commercial structures rely on fast crane mobilization, certified structural steel fabrication, and skilled rigger gangs for zero-accident erection.

Group Services Applied: Hydra (10–20T), Farana (17T), Mobile Cranes (50–80T), factory PEB shed erection, site fitters.
Key References: SIPCOT complexes, regional construction and infrastructure developers.
Sector 05

Healthcare & Hospitals

Hospitals, trauma care centers, and clinical nursing homes require unbroken, certified Medical Oxygen (I.P.) supply chains with calibrated cylinder manifolds and prompt emergency delivery capabilities.

Group Services Applied: Medical Oxygen cylinders, hospital gas pipeline support, scheduled cylinder exchange.
Compliance: Subject to state drug administration licensing and pharmacopoeial purity testing.
Sector 06

Heavy Engineering & Industrial Facilities

Large-scale fabrication shops, foundries, industrial complexes, and ship maintenance yards require comprehensive support: heavy plate rolling, pressure vessel certification, specialized TIG/MIG gases, and vetted technical manpower.

Group Services Applied: Storage tank fabrication, certified 6G welders, argon & CO2 supply, equipment shifting.
Key References: Tata Steel, Saint-Gobain, JSW, Ramco Cements.
Sector 07

Healthcare & Diagnostic Imaging

Modern clinical care, neurology, orthopaedics, and maternal-fetal medicine require advanced diagnostic clarity through planned 1.5 Tesla MRI, 32-slice CT, digital radiography, and high-resolution colour Doppler ultrasound imaging.

Group Services Applied: Roy Diagnostics advanced imaging centre, Fetal Medicine, MRI & CT diagnostics, medical oxygen supply.
Regulatory Compliance: Strict adherence to PCPNDT Act guidelines and statutory medical healthcare licensing standards.
Sector 08

Industrial Workforce & HR Management

Industrial plants, continuous process units, and large infrastructure projects require scalable, safety-trained manpower pools: mechanical fitters, certified 6G welders, pipe fabricators, plant electricians, and skilled turnaround maintenance crews.

Group Services Applied: JJ Industrial HR Services & Consultancy: Contract labour deployment, shutdown staffing, statutory compliance & payroll.
Workforce Scope: Trade-tested mechanical fitters, riggers, machinists, electrical teams, and safety supervisors.
