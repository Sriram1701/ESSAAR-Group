THE JOURNEY

Built on Faith — Driven by Vision

1995 THE FOUNDING YEAR

The remarkable journey of ESSAAR Group of Companies began in 1995, founded by the visionary couple Mr. S. Ramesh Prabakaran and Mrs. Packiabama Rameshprabakaran.

What started as an entrepreneurial vision has grown over three decades into a diversified business group, built on the strong foundations of faith, integrity, dedication, hard work and perseverance.

Through their unwavering commitment, strategic vision and determination, the founders have guided the group's growth across multiple sectors, including Engineering, Industrial Contracting, Crane & Equipment Rentals, Manpower Services, Industrial & Medical Gases, and Healthcare Diagnostics.

Their journey reflects not only business success but also a lasting commitment to employment generation, customer satisfaction, ethical business practices and community development.

Today, their entrepreneurial spirit and strong family values continue to inspire the next generation, carrying forward a legacy of excellence while embracing new opportunities for growth and innovation.

ESSAAR Group brings together seven specialized operating enterprises, functioning with mutual operational synergy while delivering dedicated expertise in their respective sectors:

  • Prateesh Engineering Enterprises: Heavy industrial structural steel fabrication, bulk liquid storage tanks (up to 1500 KL), pressure vessels, process piping, and plant shutdown maintenance.
  • SRS Cranes & Equipments Services: Modern crane rental fleet ranging from 5 to 500 Tonnes capacity, Hydra, Farana, telescopic, and crawler cranes operating out of a 1-acre SIPCOT yard.
  • Grace Gases & Company: Commercial and high-purity industrial and medical gas manufacturing, bulk cryogenic storage, and cylinder filling in Kannarapettai, Cuddalore.
  • Best Air Products: Industrial and medical gas distribution agency, manifold logistics, and cylinder fleet replenishment in Tirumalairayan Pattinam, Puducherry.
  • ESSAAR Engineering & Company: Plant mechanical piping erection, heavy equipment installation, structural assembly, and shutdown engineering contracting.
  • Roy Diagnostics Fetal & Imaging Centre: Super-specialized healthcare diagnostics featuring high-resolution 1.5T MRI, 32-slice CT, 4D Ultrasound, digital X-ray, and clinical pathology in Cuddalore.
  • JJ Industrial HR Services: Sourcing and deployment of certified industrial technicians, 6G welders, structural fitters, riggers, and compliant plant workforce management.

Yes, absolutely. Cross-functional project integration is one of ESSAAR Group's greatest competitive advantages. Industrial clients and EPC contractors can engage us under a single unified contract where:

  • Equipment, pressure vessels, tanks, and structural steel are shop-fabricated by Prateesh Engineering Enterprises.
  • Site positioning, tandem lifts, and column erections are executed by SRS Cranes & Equipments Services.
  • Continuous welding, shielding, and purging gases are supplied on-site by Grace Gases and Best Air Products.
  • Mechanical piping, equipment alignment, and turnaround contracting are completed by ESSAAR Engineering & Company.
  • Certified 6G welders, fitters, riggers, and site labor are swiftly mobilized by JJ Industrial HR Services.

This single-window accountability eliminates multiple vendor interfaces, reduces coordination delays, and delivers projects on schedule with guaranteed quality.

SRS Cranes & Equipments Services maintains an extensive fleet covering capacities from 5 to 500 Tonnes, operating from a centralized 1-acre yard in SIPCOT Industrial Area, Cuddalore:

  • Hydra Pick & Carry Cranes: 10T to 20T capacity for factory material shifting and internal plant handling.
  • Farana 4x4 Heavy Duty Cranes: 17T capacity for articulated, agile maneuvering in congested plant areas.
  • Truck-Mounted Telescopic Cranes: 20T to 25T for rapid transit, civil erection, and regional site projects.
  • Heavy Hydraulic Mobile Cranes: 50T to 80T for high-reach column lifting, tower erection, and vessel positioning.
  • High-Tonnage Project Cranes: Specialized telescopic and lattice crawler crane configurations up to 500T for major industrial lifts.

All cranes are certified with third-party load test fitness certificates and operated by trained, certified heavy-lift operators adhering to rigorous safety standards.

Grace Gases & Company and Best Air Products provide an exhaustive range of high-purity industrial and life-saving healthcare gases:

  • Industrial Gases: Industrial Oxygen (99.5%+), High-Purity Compressed & Liquid Nitrogen (up to 99.999%), Argon (TIG welding grade & ultra-pure for metallurgy), Carbon Dioxide (CO2), and Dissolved Acetylene (DA).
  • Medical Gases: Certified Medical-Grade Oxygen cylinders (pharmacopoeia compliant) for hospitals, trauma centres, ICUs, and ambulance services.
  • Logistics & Delivery: Dedicated high-pressure cylinder transport trucks, cylinder manifold exchange services, scheduled bulk replenishments, and 24/7 emergency response logistics across Cuddalore, Neyveli, Puducherry, Karaikal, and surrounding industrial belts.

Roy Diagnostics Fetal & Imaging Centre in Cuddalore is an advanced multidisciplinary diagnostic centre equipped with world-class healthcare technology:

  • 1.5 Tesla High-Resolution MRI: Whole-body, neuro, musculoskeletal, spine, and vascular imaging with superior clarity.
  • 32-Slice Low-Dose CT Scanner: High-speed volumetric imaging, coronary screenings, chest/abdominal scans, and 3D reconstructions.
  • 4D Ultrasound & Fetal Medicine: First-trimester anomaly screening, targeted anomaly scans (TIFFA), fetal Doppler, fetal echocardiography, and 3D/4D obstetrics.
  • Digital Radiography & Pathology: High-frequency Digital X-Ray, digital mammography, and fully automated clinical biochemistry, haematology, and microbiology laboratories.

JJ Industrial HR Services operates as a professional industrial human resource partner for manufacturing, petrochemical, power, and process facilities:

  • Trade-Certified Manpower: Pre-screened, certified 6G/3G welders, structural fitters, pipe fabricators, riggers, industrial electricians, and mechanical millwrights.
  • Shutdown & Turnaround Teams: Rapid mobilization of 50 to 300+ skilled personnel for planned plant maintenance turnarounds and emergency repairs.
  • 100% Statutory & Regulatory Compliance: Strict adherence to the Contract Labour Act, EPF, ESI, Factory Acts, minimum wages, and comprehensive group personal accident insurance.
  • Safety Discipline: Every technician is supplied with certified PPE and inducted on zero-incident workplace safety standards prior to site entry.

Quality assurance and workplace safety form the bedrock of all engineering and operational processes across ESSAAR Group:

  • Design & Fabrication Codes: Adherence to ASME Section VIII Div. 1, API 650 (Atmospheric Storage Tanks), ASME B31.3 (Process Piping), and IBR (Indian Boiler Regulations).
  • Non-Destructive Testing (NDT): In-house and third-party Radiographic Testing (RT), Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Magnetic Particle Testing (MPT), Liquid Penetrant Testing (DPT), and hydrostatic pressure tests.
  • Third-Party Inspection (TPI): Proven track record of successful audits and clearances by premier inspection bodies including EIL (Engineers India Limited), Bureau Veritas (BVIS), TUV Nord, Lloyd's Register, and SGS.
  • HSE Compliance: Comprehensive Health, Safety, and Environmental management system with mandatory Tool Box Talks, permit-to-work systems, and zero-incident targets.
