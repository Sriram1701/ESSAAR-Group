THE JOURNEY

Built on Faith. Driven by Vision.

1995 THE FOUNDING YEAR

The remarkable journey of ESSAAR Group of Companies began in 1995, founded by the visionary couple Mr. S. Ramesh Prabakaran and Mrs. Packiabama Rameshprabakaran.

What started as an entrepreneurial vision has grown over three decades into a diversified business group, built on the strong foundations of faith, integrity, dedication, hard work and perseverance.

Through their unwavering commitment, strategic vision and determination, the founders have guided the group's growth across multiple sectors, including Engineering, Industrial Contracting, Crane & Equipment Rentals, Manpower Services, Industrial & Medical Gases, and Healthcare Diagnostics.

Their journey reflects not only business success but also a lasting commitment to employment generation, customer satisfaction, ethical business practices and community development.

Today, their entrepreneurial spirit and strong family values continue to inspire the next generation, carrying forward a legacy of excellence while embracing new opportunities for growth and innovation.

ESSAAR Group brings together seven specialized operating units, functioning in a cohesive synergy while maintaining independent operational identities:

  • Prateesh Engineering Enterprises: Heavy industrial fabrication, storage tanks (up to 1500 KL), pressure vessels, industrial piping, and shutdown maintenance works.
  • SRS Cranes & Equipments Services: Comprehensive crane rentals (5 to 500 Tonnes capacity), Hydra, Farana, and truck-mounted heavy lifting solutions from a 1-acre SIPCOT yard.
  • Grace Gases & Company: Commercial and high-purity industrial and medical gas bottling and supply (Oxygen, Nitrogen, Argon, CO2, DA) in Kannarapettai, Cuddalore.
  • Best Air Products: Industrial and medical gas distribution, filling agency coordination, and cylinder fleet logistics in Tirumalairayan Pattinam, Puducherry.
  • ESSAAR Engineering & Company: Mechanical equipment erection, plant maintenance, piping, and certified technical workforce contracting in Pachayankuppam, Cuddalore.

Yes, absolutely. A core strength of ESSAAR Group is cross-functional project coordination. Industrial clients can engage us for integrated project execution assignments where:

  • Structural steel, tanks, or piping spools are fabricated by Prateesh Engineering Enterprises.
  • Heavy equipment erection and tandem lifts are executed by SRS Cranes & Equipments Services.
  • Continuous welding, shielding, and purging gases are supplied on-site by Grace Gases and Best Air Products.
  • Qualified fitters, riggers, and 6G welders are mobilized by ESSAAR Engineering & Company.

This single-window accountability drastically reduces procurement lead times, vendor coordination overhead, and project milestones risks.

SRS Cranes & Equipments Services maintains an expansive fleet spanning 5 to 500 Tonnes capacity operating out of an approximately 1-acre yard in SIPCOT Industrial Area, Cuddalore:

  • Hydra Pick & Carry Cranes: 10 to 20 Tonnes capacity for plant shifting and material handling.
  • Farana Cranes: 17 Tonnes capacity for articulated, agile site maneuvering.
  • Truck-Mounted Mobile Cranes: 20 to 25 Tonnes capacity for rapid regional transit and erection.
  • Heavy-Duty Mobile Hydraulic Cranes: 50 to 80 Tonnes for tall tower erection and vessel positioning.
  • Heavy Project Solutions: High-tonnage telescopic and crawler crane arrangements up to 500 Tonnes based on specific project requirements.

Grace Gases & Company and Best Air Products supply a comprehensive spectrum of certified industrial and healthcare gases:

  • Industrial Gases: Industrial Oxygen, Liquid Nitrogen, Compressed Nitrogen (purity up to 99.999%), Argon (commercial & ultra-pure for TIG welding), Carbon Dioxide (CO2), and Dissolved Acetylene (DA).
  • Medical Gases: Medical-grade Oxygen cylinders for hospitals, ICUs, emergency healthcare clinics, and ambulances.
  • Delivery & Logistics: Dedicated cylinder transport vehicles, manifold exchange services, scheduled daily/weekly replenishments, and emergency on-call supply across Cuddalore, Neyveli, Puducherry, and neighbouring districts.
