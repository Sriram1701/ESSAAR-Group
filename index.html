ESSAAR Group brings together five specialized industrial operating units, functioning in a cohesive synergy while maintaining independent operational identities:

  • 01. Prateesh Engineering Enterprises: Heavy industrial fabrication, storage tanks (up to 1500 KL), pressure vessels, industrial piping, and shutdown maintenance works.
  • 02. SRS Cranes & Equipments Services: Comprehensive crane rentals (5 to 500 Tonnes capacity), Hydra, Farana, and truck-mounted heavy lifting solutions from a 1-acre SIPCOT yard.
  • 03. Grace Gases & Company: Commercial and high-purity industrial and medical gas bottling and supply (Oxygen, Nitrogen, Argon, CO2, DA) in Kannarapettai, Cuddalore.
  • 04. Best Air Products: Industrial and medical gas distribution, filling agency coordination, and cylinder fleet logistics in Tirumalairayan Pattinam, Puducherry.
  • 05. ESSAAR Engineering & Company: Mechanical equipment erection, plant maintenance, piping, and certified technical workforce contracting in Pachayankuppam, Cuddalore.

Yes, absolutely. A core strength of ESSAAR Group is cross-functional project coordination. Industrial clients can engage us for turnkey assignments where:

  • Structural steel, tanks, or piping spools are fabricated by Prateesh Engineering Enterprises.
  • Heavy equipment erection and tandem lifts are executed by SRS Cranes & Equipments Services.
  • Continuous welding, shielding, and purging gases are supplied on-site by Grace Gases and Best Air Products.
  • Qualified fitters, riggers, and 6G welders are mobilized by ESSAAR Engineering & Company.

This single-window accountability drastically reduces procurement lead times, vendor coordination overhead, and project milestones risks.

SRS Cranes & Equipments Services maintains an expansive fleet spanning 5 to 500 Tonnes capacity operating out of an approximately 1-acre yard in SIPCOT Industrial Area, Cuddalore:

  • Hydra Pick & Carry Cranes: 10 to 20 Tonnes capacity for plant shifting and material handling.
  • Farana Cranes: 17 Tonnes capacity for articulated, agile site maneuvering.
  • Truck-Mounted Mobile Cranes: 20 to 25 Tonnes capacity for rapid regional transit and erection.
  • Heavy-Duty Mobile Hydraulic Cranes: 50 to 80 Tonnes for tall tower erection and vessel positioning.
  • Heavy Project Solutions: High-tonnage telescopic and crawler crane arrangements up to 500 Tonnes based on specific project requirements.

Grace Gases & Company and Best Air Products supply a comprehensive spectrum of certified industrial and healthcare gases:

  • Industrial Gases: Industrial Oxygen, Liquid Nitrogen, Compressed Nitrogen (purity up to 99.999%), Argon (commercial & ultra-pure for TIG welding), Carbon Dioxide (CO2), and Dissolved Acetylene (DA).
  • Medical Gases: Medical-grade Oxygen cylinders for hospitals, ICUs, emergency healthcare clinics, and ambulances.
  • Delivery & Logistics: Dedicated cylinder transport vehicles, manifold exchange services, scheduled daily/weekly replenishments, and emergency on-call supply across Cuddalore, Neyveli, Puducherry, and neighbouring districts.

Safety and quality compliance are non-negotiable standards across all five companies:

  • Fabrication QA/QC: Stage-wise inspection plans, WPS/PQR qualification, Radiographic Testing (RT), Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Liquid Penetrant Inspection (DPT), and Hydrostatic Pressure Testing up to designated design codes (ASME Section VIII, IS 2825, API 650).
  • Lifting Safety: Third-party competent person load test certification for all cranes and lifting gear, daily pre-operation checklists, certified riggers, and rigorous wind/ground bearing capacity evaluations.
  • Cylinder Safety: Statutory PESO compliance, periodic hydrostatic stretch testing, color coding, and valve safety guards.
