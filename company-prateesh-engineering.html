Enterprise Profile

Professionally Managed Mechanical Engineering Specialists

Prateesh Engineering Enterprises is a premier engineering and fabrication entity within ESSAAR Group, delivering high-precision fabrication, equipment manufacturing, and heavy erection services to leading corporations across India and overseas.

Promoted by well-experienced and technically qualified engineering professionals, the enterprise maintains complete quality control across all stages: material procurement, cut-and-roll operations, submerged arc welding (SAW), non-destructive testing (NDT/RT/UT), surface treatment, and site positioning.

Our core manufacturing hub at SIPCOT Industrial Complex, Cuddalore, features a 2,500 sq.ft covered workshop backed by a 14,000 sq.ft open heavy storage and assembly yard equipped with overhead handling systems, certified welding machines, and heavy rolling capacity.

Engineering FAQs

Technical & Project Questions

Answers regarding our tank fabrication capacities, shutdown workforce, piping standards, and quality testing procedures.

Prateesh Engineering Enterprises fabricates vertical and horizontal storage tanks up to 1500 KL capacity, raw material silos, chemical reactors, heat exchangers, and air receivers in both Carbon Steel (IS 2062, SA 516 Gr. 70) and Stainless Steel (SS 304, SS 316, SS 316L). All vessels undergo full hydraulic pressure testing and welding inspection according to ASME Section VIII and IS 2825 codes.

We execute heavy industrial piping networks (process, steam, condensate, cooling water, and compressed air) with on-site spool prefabrication, ultrasonic/radiographic weld testing, and hydro-testing. Our structural steel erection covers technological platforms, boiler support frames, conveyor galleries, and chimney flue ducts.

Yes. We maintain a mobilized technical workforce including certified 6G/TIG welders, pipe fitters, mechanical riggers, QA/QC inspectors, and safety supervisors. We regularly support annual turnarounds and emergency boiler/piping shutdown maintenance for leading clients such as Chemplast Sanmar, Tata Steel, and L&T.

Every project follows an approved Quality Assurance Plan (QAP). Raw materials arrive with certified mill test reports (MTR). Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) including Radiography (RT), Ultrasonic (UT), Magnetic Particle (MPT), and Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT) is documented and witnessed by client-appointed inspection agencies (e.g., Bureau Veritas, TUV, DNV, or client engineers).
