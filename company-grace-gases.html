Pure, Dependable Gas Solutions for Critical Demands

Grace Gases & Company stands as a trusted pillar in regional gas supply, catering to industrial chemical corridors, heavy fabrication units, infrastructure construction sites, and premier healthcare facilities across Cuddalore and neighbouring districts.

We recognize that whether supplying high-purity argon for TIG welding in a pressure vessel fabrication shop or delivering critical medical-grade oxygen to emergency hospital wards, purity, continuity, and pressure safety are paramount.

Every cylinder distributed through our Kannarapettai facility undergoes pre-fill valve inspection, tare weight verification, moisture purge protocols, and leak detection testing.

Our Gas Portfolio

Products Supplied

Comprehensive inventory of essential industrial, shielding, cutting, and life-support gases.

Industrial Oxygen (O₂)

Essential for oxy-fuel cutting, structural steel profiling, brazing, and high-temperature furnace operations in heavy fabrication shops.

  • High combustion efficiency
  • Standard 7 m³ high-pressure cylinders

Medical Oxygen (I.P.)

Ultra-pure breathing oxygen compliant with Indian Pharmacopoeia standards for intensive care units, emergency ambulances, and clinical nursing homes.

  • Dedicated medical cylinder stock
  • Batch tested purity compliance

Nitrogen Gas (N₂)

Inert blanketing gas used for purging chemical pipelines, pressure testing vessel lines, pharmaceutical processing, and laser cutting.

  • Dry, moisture-free inert atmosphere
  • High pressure pneumatic testing

Argon Gas (Ar)

High-purity shielding gas indispensable for TIG (GTAW) and MIG (GMAW) welding of stainless steel, aluminum, alloy steels, and piping systems.

  • 99.99%+ purity for radiographic welding
  • Minimal spatter & pristine weld seams

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

Standard shielding gas for structural MIG welding, foundry core manufacturing, fire safety systems, and pH neutralization in wastewater plants.

  • Economical deep penetration welding
  • Reliable liquid & vapor withdrawal

Dissolved Acetylene (DA)

High-temperature fuel gas delivering an intense 3,100°C flame temperature for heavy plate cutting, gouging, preheating, and flame hardening.

  • Maximum thermal cutting velocity
  • Specially tested porous mass cylinders
