Enterprise Profile

Premier Industrial Manpower & HR Management Solutions

JJ Industrial HR Services & Consultancy is the specialized human capital and manpower deployment division of ESSAAR Group, delivering certified, tested, and safety-trained technical personnel to industrial manufacturing facilities, fabrication yards, chemical processing plants, and shutdown maintenance operations across South India.

With an extensive active talent registry of skilled mechanical tradesmen, high-pressure 6G welders, structural fitters, electrical technicians, and project supervisors, we provide agile workforce solutions tailored to continuous plant operations as well as rapid turnaround shutdown projects.

Every deployed technician undergoes rigorous background verification, competency evaluation, and mandatory occupational health and safety induction to ensure seamless integration and zero-incident workplace performance.
