Three Decades of Multi-Sector Industrial Capability

Founded in 1995 in Cuddalore, Tamil Nadu, ESSAAR Group of Companies is a diversified industrial conglomerate bringing together specialized engineering fabrication, heavy crane rental, pure industrial gases, healthcare diagnostics, and certified workforce management across South India.

— OUR FOUNDERS & LEGACY —

Rooted in Values. Building the Future.

Founded in 1995 by Mr. S. Ramesh Prabakaran and Mrs. Packiabama Rameshprabakaran, ESSAAR Group was built on the pillars of faith, integrity and entrepreneurial vision.

For more than three decades, their leadership has nurtured a culture of excellence, creating opportunities, empowering people and delivering value across industries.

Their legacy continues to inspire the next generation as we grow with purpose and responsibility.
