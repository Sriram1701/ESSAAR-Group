THE JOURNEY

Built on Faith. Driven by Vision.

1995 THE FOUNDING YEAR

The remarkable journey of ESSAAR Group of Companies began in 1995, founded by the visionary couple Mr. S. Ramesh Prabakaran and Mrs. Packiabama Rameshprabakaran.

What started as an entrepreneurial vision has grown over three decades into a diversified business group, built on the strong foundations of faith, integrity, dedication, hard work and perseverance.

Through their unwavering commitment, strategic vision and determination, the founders have guided the group's growth across multiple sectors, including Engineering, Industrial Contracting, Crane & Equipment Rentals, Manpower Services, Industrial & Medical Gases, and Healthcare Diagnostics.

Their journey reflects not only business success but also a lasting commitment to employment generation, customer satisfaction, ethical business practices and community development.

Today, their entrepreneurial spirit and strong family values continue to inspire the next generation, carrying forward a legacy of excellence while embracing new opportunities for growth and innovation.

Standards of Execution

Quality Assurance & HSE Culture

Safety and precision are engineered into every protocol, procedure, and site dispatch across all group subsidiaries.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Benchmarks

Our fabrication and engineering facilities follow rigorous ISO quality management systems, complete with raw material test reports (MTR), WPS/PQR certifications, and hydrostatic pressure records.

Zero-Incident Safety Protocols

Every heavy crane lift, high-pressure gas cylinder transfer, and shutdown maintenance task is governed by daily toolbox safety briefings, risk assessments, and PPE compliance.

Continuous Workforce Upskilling

Our welders, fitters, crane operators, and rigging personnel undergo regular trade qualification tests and safety refreshers to ensure flawless execution in active chemical plants and refineries.
