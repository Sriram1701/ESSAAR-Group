Three Decades of Multi-Sector Industrial Capability

Founded in 1995 in Cuddalore, Tamil Nadu, ESSAAR Group of Companies is a diversified industrial conglomerate bringing together specialized engineering fabrication, heavy crane rental, pure industrial gases, healthcare diagnostics, and certified workforce management across South India.

— OUR FOUNDERS & LEGACY —

Rooted in Values — Building the Future

Founded in 1995 by Mr. S. Ramesh Prabakaran and Mrs. Packiabama Rameshprabakaran, ESSAAR Group was built on the enduring pillars of faith, integrity, and entrepreneurial vision.

For more than three decades, their stewardship has nurtured a culture of operational excellence—creating meaningful opportunities, empowering people, and delivering lasting value across industries.

Their legacy continues to guide and inspire the next generation as we grow forward with clarity, purpose, and unwavering responsibility.
